Delete your OMNI account and data

In the app

  1. Open OMNI and tap the account icon (top right).
  2. Tap Delete account and data and confirm.

This erases your account, usage history and subscription records from our servers.

Without the app

Email @@EMAIL@@ from the address linked to your account with the subject "Delete my OMNI account". We will confirm and delete your data.

Good to know

  • Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  • Files you scanned, compressed or hid in the Vault are only on your device. Uninstalling OMNI removes them.
