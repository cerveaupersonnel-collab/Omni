What stays on your device

Photos, videos and documents you scan, compress, clean or hide in the Vault are processed on your device. We do not upload them. Text recognition (OCR) and the document scanner run on your device through Google ML Kit. Vault files are stored in the app's private storage and unlocked with your fingerprint, face or screen lock.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account and all server-side data in the app (Account & privacy > Delete account and data) or on this page. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play. Usage counters are removed automatically after a few days.

Children

OMNI is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to server data is restricted by security rules and server-side checks.
