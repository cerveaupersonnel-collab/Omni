# Store listing draft (English)

**App name:** OMNI: Scan, PDF Chat, Cleaner
**Short description (max 80):** Scan, PDF chat, cleaner, video compressor and a private vault in one app.

**Full description**
OMNI replaces a stack of single-purpose apps with one fast, private app.

- Scan: capture documents with automatic edge detection and cropping, read the text on your device, export PDF or JPG.
- Chat with PDF: ask questions, get summaries, translations and tables from any PDF up to 50 MB.
- Clean: find duplicate and blurry photos, large videos and WhatsApp media. You approve every deletion.
- Video Compressor: shrink videos for WhatsApp Status or TikTok while keeping them sharp.
- Secure Vault: hide photos and videos behind your fingerprint or face, outside your gallery.

Free: 3 uses per feature per day, with ads. OMNI Pro: unlimited use, no ads and priority AI. Subscriptions renew until canceled in Google Play.
